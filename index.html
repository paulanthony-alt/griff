FAQ

Good to know

Anything else, call the Outing Director at 203-531-6176.

How many players do we need?

Shotgun outings have an 80-player minimum, so the whole field tees off at once and finishes together. Planning for fewer? Send an inquiry anyway and the Outing Director will go over what's possible for your group.

When in the season can we book?

Outings run during the golf season with a 12:30–1:00 pm shotgun start. Popular dates book early, so include a backup date in your inquiry.

What does it cost?

Golf is $88 per player: a $68 green fee plus a $20 cart. Food, beverage and prizes are quoted separately based on what you choose.

Is there a deposit?

Deposit and payment terms are confirmed in writing when you reserve your date. The Outing Director will walk you through them before anything is final.

What happens if it rains?

Golf goes ahead in light rain. If the course closes for weather or lightning, the Outing Director will work with you on next steps. The weather terms for your outing are spelled out when you book.

Can we bring our own food or prizes?

Food and beverage are handled by Clubhouse at the Griff, the on-site restaurant, and prizes are available through the Pro Shop. Mention any sponsor gifts or special requests in your inquiry.
